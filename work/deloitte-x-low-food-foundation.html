Deloitte x Low Food Foundation: Net Positive Network & Partner Program

We partnered with Deloitte, the Low Food Foundation and Amsterdam creative agency Havas Lemz to design and develop two Webflow platforms that support their food waste initiatives, their partner community and their education programs. Both websites were delivered as fully handover ready systems now maintained independently by the organisations.

Dale Wesdorp

•

March 13, 2026

Share

The challenge

The problem
behind the brief

Deloitte and the Low Food Foundation were launching new initiatives that address food waste and promote more sustainable practices across the food system. They needed two digital platforms that could communicate their mission clearly and host community activity, events and signups for their Young Academy programs. These platforms had to align with the brand identity created by Havas Lemz, while being built in Webflow so the organisations could eventually take full ownership of content and ongoing updates.

Our approach

How we built the right thing, and built it right

Design

Using the visual identity created by Havas Lemz, we created flexible layouts and components that support storytelling and educational content. Working in collaboration with a branding agency allowed us to focus on digital clarity and user experience while ensuring full alignment with the creative direction of the initiative.

Development

We developed both platforms in Webflow using custom CMS structures designed for events, programs and other recurring content. Our focus was on creating systems the teams could own, update and scale without external support. Today, Deloitte and the Low Food Foundation manage and evolve the websites independently, showing the long term value of choosing Webflow for a project like this.

Strategy

We worked closely with Deloitte and the Low Food Foundation to translate their brand, content and user experience requirements into a clear information architecture. Our goal was to create platforms that visitors could navigate intuitively, making it easy to understand the mission, join events and participate in the growing community.
