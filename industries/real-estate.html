From asset to platform

In a world where space becomes service, we help real estate organisations build digital products that respond to flexible demand, data-driven operations and new business models.

Snapshot

Industry snapshot

The modern real-estate industry is undergoing a fundamental change. Traditional models of long-term leases, static occupancy and siloed operations are being disrupted by technology, changing work patterns and shifting consumer expectations. Across commercial and residential sectors, companies face pressure on every front: sustainability targets are no longer optional, tenant demands are accelerating, and digital real-estate platforms and fully digital experiences are the expected new normal. PwC+2proprli.com+2

In parallel, users expect everything from booking a space on-demand to seamless mobile experiences and full transparency around services and operations. According to industry data, ~72 % of property seekers now want digital transaction support and ~66 % of home searches begin on mobile devices. Gitnux

For real-estate owners, operators and developers the challenge is clear: adapt or risk under-utilised assets, margin erosion, and outdated business models. Yet legacy systems, fragmented data, and outdated ERPs remain widespread. Many organisations know they must modernise but struggle to unify operations, user experience, and technology into a coherent transformation.

At the same time, the opportunity space is there: flexible occupancy, smart building platforms and service-oriented real-estate are creating new revenue streams.

Opportunities

Tech & Product opportunities

We believe the real-estate sector is ripe for products that deliver three kinds of value: flexibility, insight, and service-as-space. Here’s how we see the territory:

Flexible occupancy platforms

Booking and management engines for hourly/daily usage, hybrid workflows, mixed-use spaces – enabling real-estate to behave like a service rather than a static asset.

Data-driven operations and analytics

Dashboards and decision systems that analyse occupancy, energy, utilisation, user behaviour – helping owners optimise assets and status in real-time.

Smart building services and connected ecosystems

IoT sensors, automation, mobile access, user-apps – buildings that adapt to tenants and provide richer experiences.

Digitised leasing & marketplace experiences

Mobile-first portals for discovery, booking, leasing; digital contracts; fractional and marketplace models that open new segments. Forbes+1

Sustainability & compliance tools

Platforms enabling real-estate owners to meet net-zero and efficiency targets, handle regulatory workflows, monitor ESG metrics. PwC+1

Miyagami's POV

What most teams get wrong

Many real-estate teams know they need to “go digital”, but often miss three critical traps:

Starting with features instead of value

Building a “cool app” or “smart building dashboard” without first defining the user experience or business model often leads to adoption failure.

Treating space like it used to be

The assumption that long leases and static usage will continue ignores changing occupier behaviour and flexible demand.

Neglecting operational readiness

Technology is only as good as the processes, data and culture behind it. Without change management, the investment falls flat.

Our approach is grounded, product-centric and execution-focused:
